Purpose of the website

This website introduces the Massaly Lab and provides general information about its research, publications, and people. Please use it responsibly and in accordance with applicable laws.

Research summaries are intended for general education. They are not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations and do not establish a clinician–patient relationship. Refer to the linked publications for study methods, findings, and limitations.
