Open positions

In addition to our lab, the Department of Anesthesiology, the David Geffen School of Medicine, and UCLA will provide an outstanding environment for collaborative opportunities and support for your professional and personal development.

01

Research Technician / Lab Manager

We are looking to recruit a Research Technician / Lab Manager!

Responsibilities include animal colony management and animal protocol updates, as well as routine molecular and cellular biological procedures such as immunohistochemistry, western blotting, and tissue fixation.

Experience in rodent handling and a strong interest in learning behavioral neuroscience approaches is preferred.

The candidate will also be responsible for lab administrative tasks, including:

  • Maintaining and ensuring compliance with laboratory safety regulations.
  • Managing equipment inventory and keeping laboratory supplies, including PPE and reagents, sufficiently stocked.
  • Organizing lab meetings and journal clubs, supporting communication, and training new personnel.
  • Helping maintain the lab’s computer network and manage data acquisition, storage, and analysis from major equipment.

Please attach your CV and cover letter if you are interested in this position.

02

Post doctoral Position(s)

Postdoctoral positions are available in the lab!

We are looking for motivated, creative, and enthusiastic members to join the lab for their postdoctoral training.

If you are looking for a lab environment to build on your previous research expertise, acquire novel technical skills and knowledge in pain, affect, and/or substance use disorders, and work with people committed to helping you pursue your professional career path, we should definitely chat!

Successful candidates should have a strong background in one or more of the following areas:

  • Rodent behavior
  • Optogenetic or chemogenetic approaches
  • Pharmacology
  • Calcium imaging
  • Biochemistry
