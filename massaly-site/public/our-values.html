Our Values

Our Commitment For Your Research Goals

The Massaly Lab, together with the whole Department of Anesthesiology and Perioperative Medicine at UCLA, is committed to celebrating and supporting scientists from all backgrounds, ethnicities, nationalities, ages, religions, gender identities, and sexual orientations to develop into a team that values promoting its peers.

With equity in training, career development, and opportunities for all, our lab will provide you a creative, safe, positive, and nurturing environment to pursue your own ideas, shape your future independent research, and thrive in your personal and professional endeavors.

We believe that diversity is not only a necessary means for all sciences to develop and flourish, but a crucial pillar for the advancement of our institutions and societies, and for building a stronger and fairer future together.

Our door is and will always be open.
