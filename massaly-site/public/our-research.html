Our Research

Understanding the neural changes that connect pain, emotional states, and behavior.

Improving life
beyond pain.

Quality of life for patients living with pain is affected by co-occurring conditions, including prolonged negative emotional states that can contribute to depression, drug misuse, and life-threatening events.

Our lab uses multiple avenues to improve pain treatment and reduce the development of opioid use disorder.

OUR METHODS

A multidisciplinary
approach.

We work to unravel the neuronal networks and allostatic changes—adaptations to persistent challenges—that may influence behavioral outcomes.

Our approaches include viral-based neurocircuitry tracing, pharmacology, optogenetics and chemogenetics, and behavioral models of pain, negative emotional states, and drug self-administration.
