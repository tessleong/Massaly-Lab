PAIN / REWARD / MOTIVATION

OUR APPROACH

Pain is more than
a sensation.

Pain can reshape how we experience rewards, respond to our surroundings, and pursue the things that matter to us.

At the Massaly Lab, we investigate the neural changes behind these experiences. Our work brings together the study of brain circuits, behavior, and the body's own opioid systems to understand the relationship between pain, emotional states, and substance use.

We combine molecular biology, pharmacology, neurophysiology, and behavioral neuroscience to connect changes in cells and circuits with changes in behavior.
