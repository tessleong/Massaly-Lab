Privacy

How information is handled when you visit this website.

Last updated October 5, 2026

About this notice

This notice describes this Massaly Lab website. Linked websites, including the existing lab website, university websites, and journal websites, may have different privacy practices.

Browsing this site

The lab pages do not include contact forms, newsletter registration, advertising pixels, or lab-added analytics. Their code does not set cookies or store information in your browser. The lab’s images, fonts, and page assets are served with the site. The embedded location map is provided by Google Maps.

Hosting and access

This site is hosted through OpenAI Sites. The hosting and sign-in services may process technical information, such as IP addresses, browser details, access logs, and authentication information, and may use cookies needed to provide and secure access. These services operate separately from the lab page code. See OpenAI’s privacy policy for information about its data practices and available choices.

Contacting the lab

Email links open your email application. If you send a message, the recipient receives your email address and the information you choose to include. University email handling is subject to applicable UCLA policies. Please do not send sensitive medical information through general inquiries.

External websites and university information

The embedded map connects to Google when it loads. Google may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details and may use cookies under its own privacy policy.

Following a link takes you to a separately operated website whose own policies apply. For university privacy information and contacts, visit UCLA’s Privacy Policy and Terms of Use. This notice does not replace university policies.

Questions and updates

For questions about this lab website, contact nmassaly@mednet.ucla.edu. This notice may be updated as the site changes; the date above identifies the latest revision.
